# Adam Pay — build notes

Personal Android cash wallet (Jordanian Dinar, cash only). Sibling of Hyper Athlete v2; reuses its architecture,
not its look. Built phase by phase from the Adam Pay brief.

## Target phone
- Honor X7e (LAB-LX2), MagicOS 10 (Android 16), Helio G81 Ultra, 6 GB RAM, 720×1604 at 120 Hz.
- AI Button: Hyper Athlete keeps single press; Adam Pay will take double press (phase 6, launch router).

## Decisions taken
- Kotlin + Compose, MVVM, Hilt, Room (DB v1), DataStore, WorkManager. Package `com.adampay.app`.
- Same toolchain as Hyper Athlete: AGP 9.4.1, Kotlin 2.4.21, Compose BOM 2026.09.00, Glance 1.2.0 (later),
  play-services-auth 21.4.0 (later, not 22.0.0). compileSdk 37 / targetSdk 36 / minSdk 26. arm64-v8a only.
- **Own release key** (user's choice, 2026-10-17): `keystore/adampay-release.p12` (PKCS12, RSA 4096, alias `adampay`,
  valid 100 years). Random passwords in `keystore/keystore.properties`. `keystore/` is git-ignored; a copy was sent to the
  user once for safekeeping. Cert SHA-1 `A0:5B:9F:E8:38:D7:51:BF:8C:76:40:B2:FF:E6:E7:B4:5A:B6:D7:A7`.
  Phase 5 needs an Android OAuth client in Google Cloud: package `com.adampay.app` + that SHA-1.
- Local git only, no remote (user's choice).
- Money: every amount is a `Long` in fils (1 JD = 1000 fils, 1 qirsh = 10 fils). No Float/Double anywhere.
  Balances are never stored: they are derived from the `txn` table (`WalletDao.observeBalances`, mirrored by the pure
  `Ledger.balances` and tested against each other).
- Display: Latin digits, default 3 decimals + "JD"; settings for 2 decimals (single fils still show all 3) and "د.أ".
  Real minus sign (U+2212); numbers wrapped in `Bidi.ltr`, mixed labels in `Bidi.isolate` (Hyper Athlete's `Bidi`).
- Week starts Saturday (setting: Saturday / Sunday / Monday). Used from phase 3 (weekly budget).
- Language modes as in Hyper Athlete: MIXED (default) = Arabic interface + English category/pocket names,
  ARABIC, ENGLISH. Instant switch, no restart (`ProvideAppLanguage`). Arabic copy: formal MSA (see Design).
- Txn types: EXPENSE, INCOME, TRANSFER, LOST, FOUND + **OPENING** (starting cash from the first-run screen; counts in
  balances, never in income stats). Direction: EXPENSE/LOST use `from_pocket_id`, INCOME/FOUND/OPENING use
  `to_pocket_id`, TRANSFER uses both.
- Categories with history are archived, unused ones deleted (old rows keep their names).
- Seeds: pockets في جيبي / In my pocket, في المنزل / Hidden at home, الحصّالة / Piggy bank (green gradients);
  8 expense + 5 income categories from the brief (formal Arabic names); 3 example quick items
  (Service taxi 0.350, Falafel sandwich 0.500, Top-up 1.000). Untouched 0.1.0 seeds are renamed/recoloured on start.
- Room schema v1 already has every table of the brief (cash_count, weekly_budget, goal, debt) so phases 2–3 don't
  need a migration unless their design changes.

## Design (user feedback 2026-10-17: formal, bank-like, dark + green)
- Dark is the default: near-black with a faint green tint `#080C0A`, deep green-grey surfaces, emerald accent
  `#2FB36D`. Light mode in the same greens. Accent choices: emerald, mint, teal green, leaf, then sky, lavender,
  gold, rose. Expense = clay red, income = mint, transfer = slate; always with a sign/icon.
- **No name or logo inside the app** (user's request). The launcher icon is an original green wallet with a card strap
  and a side clasp (same idea as the user's reference picture), on a dark green background; it also shows on the splash.
- Formal copy everywhere (Modern Standard Arabic, bank vocabulary: الرصيد الإجمالي، كشف الحساب، الرصيد الافتتاحي).
  The brief's friendly Jordanian tone is superseded for the interface (the phase 7 assistant can still speak Jordanian).
- Home: greeting + the user's name, a balance card (total + Expense / Income / Statement actions), a Pockets box with
  **one card at a time** (swipe; page dots), Today, quick entries, recent transactions. Full-width bottom bar:
  Home · Statement · + · Settings.
- Amounts use **Adam Figures**: Tajawal with tabular digits, generated by `tools/make_figures.py` (OFL, renamed).
- Strings come from one table: `tools/strings.py` writes `values/` (English) and `values-ar/`.
- Icons: Material Symbols Rounded (Apache-2.0) converted with `tools/svg2vd.py`.

## Build environment notes
- Network: dl.google.com, maven.google.com, repo.maven.apache.org, plugins.gradle.org, services.gradle.org.
  Maven Central sometimes answers 429 on a cold cache; retry (artifacts already fetched stay cached).
- `ANDROID_HOME=/home/claude/android-sdk` (cmdline-tools, platforms;android-37.0, build-tools 37.0.0).
- Run `testDebugUnitTest` and `recordRoborazziDebug` in separate Gradle calls. Screenshots at 360×802 dp.
- No emulator; device testing on the phone.

## Phase 1 — 0.1.0-phase1, versionCode 1, commit e74b632 (2026-10-17)
- **Foundation:** Hilt app (`AdamApp` seeds defaults on start), Room v1 (all brief tables), DataStore settings,
  launcher router `.MainActivity` (no UI; brings the open app back or starts `.HomeActivity`) — phase 6 adds the AI Button.
- **Home:** wordmark + hide-amounts eye, total balance (digit roll), pocket cards row (tap → History filtered to that
  pocket), Today card (spent / came in / entries), quick-add chips (one tap logs + Undo snackbar), Recent (5).
  Sections reorderable / hideable (Settings → Appearance → Arrange Home), saved as `layout_HOME`.
- **Add sheet:** Expense / Income toggle, amount on the custom keypad (max 999,999.999, long-press ⌫ clears),
  category grid (two rows, row-major, scrolls sideways past 8), pocket chip (default pocket), date chip (date picker),
  note. Save → snackbar with Undo. The same sheet edits a row (delete button). No category picked = "Other".
- **History:** grouped by day with day out/in totals, type chips (All / Expenses / Income / Transfers / Lost & found),
  category and pocket filters, note + category search (Arabic-aware: أ/إ/آ=ا, ة=ه, ى=ي, no diacritics),
  swipe to delete with Undo, tap to edit.
- **Settings:** Appearance (live preview, theme System/Light/Dark, 8 accents, text size S/M/L, hide amounts, arrange
  Home, reset), Language (3 modes), Amount display (2/3 decimals, JD / د.أ, week start), default pocket, vibration,
  Categories (Expense/Income tabs, drag to reorder, editor with AR/EN names, 45 icons, 12 colours, remove = archive if
  used), Quick items (drag to reorder, editor with name, type, amount keypad, category, pocket or default).
- **First run:** welcome + language, then "how much cash do you have now?" per pocket (keypad) → OPENING rows. Skip allowed.
- Bug caught by the screenshots before release: settings rows ran their click as trailing *content*; `SettingsRow` now
  takes `onClick` last so a trailing lambda is always the click.
- Tests: 31 unit/Robolectric tests (fils maths + formatting, keypad, ledger, history filter/search, layout, Room
  seeding / balance SQL vs pure ledger / undo / archive / reorder, end-to-end smoke test) + 30 Roborazzi screenshots
  (Home, add sheet, History, Settings pages, editors, first run; dark/light, mixed/Arabic/English).
- Release APK ≈ 2.0 MB, signed with the Adam Pay key (verified: SHA-1 A0:5B:9F:…:D7:A7).
- Unverified on device: 120 Hz feel and the digit roll, haptics strength, add-sheet note field with the keyboard open,
  date picker in Arabic, swipe-to-delete direction in Arabic, MagicOS font scaling with "Large" text.

## Phase 2 — 0.2.0-phase2, versionCode 4, commit 1d71eac (2026-10-17)
(0.2.0-phase2-preview, versionCode 3, commit 20405af, was sent early at the user's request before tests ran.)
- **Cash count** (`ui/cash/CountScreen`, pure `domain/cash/CashCount.kt`): pick the pocket, count notes and coins with
  steppers (note = rounded rectangle, coin = circle) or type the amount, live total → Compare.
  Match → save. Missing → "record as expense" (amount defaults to what's left, category, note; several allowed) and/or
  "lost / don't remember" for the rest. Extra → "record as income" and/or "found money". Nothing is written until
  **Save count**; `WalletRepository.saveCount` writes the `cash_count` row (expected, counted, denominations JSON) and
  the EXPENSE / LOST / INCOME / FOUND rows (`created_by = COUNT`, `cash_count_id`) in one transaction, so the pocket's
  balance then equals the counted cash. Leaving mid-count asks before discarding. "Lost this month" shows on a missing result.
- **Denominations:** default 50, 20, 10, 5, 1 JD, ½, ¼ JD, 10, 5, 1 qirsh; Settings → Money → Notes and coins
  (remove / add / restore defaults), DataStore key `denominations`.
- **Pockets:** tap a card on Home → sheet with the card, last count ("Last count: yesterday, amount missing"),
  Count cash, Transfer, Pocket statement, Edit. Settings → Lists → Pockets: drag to reorder, new pocket, editor with
  Arabic/English names, 12 icons, 12 gradients or a custom colour (hue + brightness bars; end colour = same hue darker),
  live card preview. Remove: refused while the pocket holds money or when it's the last one; archived if it has history
  (restore from the Archived list), deleted if never used.
- **Transfers:** third tab "Transfer" in the add sheet (From / To rows, swapping when the same pocket is picked, warning
  when the amount is above the source balance); editable from the statement. Total balance never changes.
- Count reminder every N days moves to Phase 3 with the other notifications.
- Tests: 50 unit/Robolectric (new: CashCountTest; DB: count + lost, found, match, transfer, pocket removal rules,
  pocket order; smoke test now also counts cash and marks the difference lost) + 44 screenshots.
- Unverified on device: stepper feel and haptics during a long count, the custom colour bars by finger, the pocket sheet
  over the pager swipe, Arabic layout of the From/To rows.

## Phase 3 — 0.3.0-phase3, versionCode 5, commit 5f9d315 (2026-10-17)
- **Debts dropped at the user's request** (2026-10-17). The `debt` table from schema v1 stays unused (no migration);
  no debt code, screens or transaction types.
- **Weekly budget** (`domain/plan/Planning.kt` `BudgetStatus`, `ui/plan/PlanUi.kt`): Home section + editor sheet
  (keypad, remove), also from Settings → Planning. Week from the user's first day (default Saturday). Only EXPENSE rows
  count (lost money, transfers, opening cash don't). Shows left, ≈ per day for the remaining days (including today),
  days left, progress bar (accent → warn above 80% → red when over), spent / limit. Setting a new limit deactivates the
  old row and inserts one from this week's start (history kept for stats).
- **Savings goals:** Home section (first two) + Goals page (drag to reorder, new, editor sheet): name, target (keypad),
  optional linked pocket (progress = pocket balance), optional target date, 16 icons, 12 colours, delete. Goals without
  a pocket: "Add or take out money" (never below zero). Progress line: reached / date passed / "save X a week" (with a
  date) / "at this pace: date" (pace = pocket net inflow over 28 days, or saved ÷ days since creation) / no estimate.
- **Logging streak:** days with an entry the user made (by `created_at`; opening cash and count adjustments don't
  count) or a "nothing spent today" tap (DataStore `no_spend_days`, last 400 days). Chip on the Today card; today not
  yet logged keeps the streak. "I spent nothing today" button on the Today card when nothing is logged.
- **Reminders** (`core/reminders`): evening "What did you spend today?" (default on, 21:30, Settings → Reminders →
  time picker) with RemoteInput "Enter amount" (numbers; saved as EXPENSE / Other in the default pocket,
  `created_by = NOTIFICATION`; bad input → the reminder comes back with an error line) and "Nothing spent" action.
  After a reply the notification turns into "Saved X — tap to choose the category" → `QuickCategoryActivity`
  (translucent sheet over any app: category grid + note). Optional cash-count reminder: daily check at 19:00, fires when
  no pocket was counted for N days (default 7; opens the cash count). Inexact `AlarmManager.setWindow` (10 min, no
  exact-alarm permission), re-armed after each firing, on settings changes (`AdamApp`), boot, app update, time/zone
  change (`BootReceiver`). Notification permission: asked once after setup (Android 13+) and from the Reminders page,
  which also explains MagicOS background launch.
- **Quick entries settings** (user request): swipe right = delete (snackbar Undo restores the same id), swipe left =
  open the editor; physical screen directions in Arabic too (`SwipeEditDelete`). Swipe actions now run once per
  gesture (also History's swipe-to-delete).
- Permissions: POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED.
- Tests: 61 unit/Robolectric (new: PlanningTest — week, budget, goal pace/eta/per week, streak, reminder timing and
  count-due; DB: budget rows, goals; QuickSwipeTest — right deletes / left edits in English and Arabic) + 53 screenshots.
- Unverified on device: notification inline reply on MagicOS, the reminder arriving with battery optimisation on,
  the quick category sheet over another app, time picker in Arabic, swipe feel in the quick entries list.

## Phase 5 — 0.5.0-phase5, versionCode 6, commit 0a38252 (2026-10-17)
- **App lock** (`core/security/AppLock.kt`, `domain/security/Security.kt`, `ui/security/PinUi.kt`): 4-digit PIN kept
  only as PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (120 000 iterations, 16-byte random salt, constant-time compare). Five free tries, then
  30 s, 1 min, 2 min … up to 15 min (wrong-try count and wait survive closing the app). Lock again after leaving:
  immediately (default) / 1 min / 5 min (`LockPolicy`, `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime`; HomeActivity onStop/onStart,
  ignoring configuration changes; a new process always starts locked). Settings → Account and security → App lock:
  on/off (setting a PIN twice; turning off needs the PIN), change PIN (current first), fingerprint, timeout.
  "Forgot PIN?" explains that it can't be recovered (clear storage + restore from Drive).
- Lock screen: "Welcome back, <name>", dots, round keypad with a fingerprint key; drawn in its **own full-screen dialog
  window** (so it also covers sheets/dialogs that were open) plus an opaque cover in the main window (no frame of the
  wallet before the lock window). Back sends the app to the background. Returning from Google's sign-in screen doesn't
  ask again (`AppLock.expectReturn`).
- **Fingerprint:** platform `android.hardware.biometrics.BiometricPrompt` (Android 11+; BIOMETRIC_STRONG, else WEAK),
  so no androidx.biometric (its latest is an alpha) and no FragmentActivity. Turning it on asks for the finger once.
  The prompt opens by itself on the lock screen. `USE_BIOMETRIC` permission.
- **Hide in recent apps:** optional FLAG_SECURE on HomeActivity (blank recents thumbnail, screenshots blocked;
  sheets and dialogs inherit it). "Hide amounts" is also on the same page.
- Lock settings live in their own DataStore (`security`): never backed up or restored.
- **Google account** (`core/auth/GoogleAuth.kt`, Hyper Athlete's approach): Identity `AuthorizationClient`, scopes
  openid / email / profile / `drive.appdata`. Name + e-mail from the OpenID userinfo endpoint. **No token stored**:
  each backup asks Play services for a fresh one silently. Sign out revokes the permission (best effort) and forgets the
  account on the phone; backups stay in Drive. Account + backup status in their own DataStore (`account`).
- **Drive backup** (`core/backup/`, ported from Hyper Athlete): `SnapshotStore` reads every Room table straight from
  SQLite (500-row chunks) + all settings; `BackupPlan` gzips content-addressed chunks (`c-<sha256>.gz`) + a manifest
  (`m-<time>.json`) in the hidden appDataFolder, so a backup uploads only changed chunks and an unchanged day adds no
  backup. 3 GB rule: only when over 3 GB, backups older than 30 days go oldest first; the newest always stays.
  Restore replaces everything in one transaction (refuses other apps' or newer-schema files).
- Settings → Account and security → Backup: sign in, last backup, space used + number of backups, last error
  (setup / sign in again / offline / failed), Back up now, Back up every night (23:30, `BackupWorker`, needs internet,
  retried with back-off, skipped before setup is finished), Restore → pick a backup (newest first) → warning → restore.
  Right after signing in on a phone that never backed up, if Drive already has backups it offers to restore the newest.
- Dependencies: play-services-auth 21.4.0, okhttp 5.5.0 (+ mockwebserver3 for tests). Permissions: INTERNET,
  ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, USE_BIOMETRIC. Settings got one new group (Account and security) after Profile; nothing else
  moved.
- **Google Cloud set-up needed for sign-in** (user does this): OAuth consent screen (External, testing, add the Gmail as
  test user, scope `.../auth/drive.appdata`), Google Drive API enabled, OAuth client type **Android**: package
  `com.adampay.app`, SHA-1 `A0:5B:9F:E8:38:D7:51:BF:8C:76:40:B2:FF:E6:E7:B4:5A:B6:D7:A7`. Until then sign-in shows
  "Google sign-in isn't set up for this app yet (Code 10)".
- Tests: 81 unit/Robolectric + 63 screenshots. New: SecurityBackupTest (PIN hash/salt/verify, throttle, lock timing,
  chunk encode/decode, incremental names, 3 GB rule, 23:30 delay); BackupFlowTest (whole backup → change → restore older
  → wipe → restore newest against a fake Drive server over HTTP with real multipart uploads; schema version; foreign
  files refused; AppLock wrong-PIN counting/wait/reset); AppSmokeTest now also sets a PIN, leaves the app, comes back to
  the lock screen, is refused a wrong PIN and opens with the right one.
- Unverified on device: Google sign-in and Drive calls (need the OAuth client), the fingerprint prompt on MagicOS, the
  nightly worker under MagicOS battery rules, FLAG_SECURE thumbnail on MagicOS recents.

## Phase 6 — 0.6.0-phase6, versionCode 7, commit 2e616c0 (2026-10-17)
- **Phase 7 (assistant) dropped at the user's request** (2026-10-17): no voice assistant, no Gemini. Phase 6 is the last
  planned phase.
- **Featured goal card** (user request): a pink card right under the total balance (new home box `HOME_FEATURED`, first
  in the order; saved layouts get it inserted at the top; hideable in "Arrange home"). Shows the goal's icon and name,
  saved / target, percent, a four-level segmented bar (levels at 25/50/75/100 %, "Level 2/4"), and what's left or the
  weekly amount needed. The bar fills when shown with a soft shine running across it (no animation in screenshot tests).
  **Original design**: the user asked for a GTA 6 / Vice City look; that's the game's own visual identity, so it was
  declined and an original pink design was made instead (pink → magenta → plum gradient, fine diagonal lines, a
  four-point sparkle). Which goal: `featured_goal` setting — absent = the first goal not yet reached, 0 = none, else
  that goal; goal editor switch "Show at the top of Home". The Home goals list leaves out the featured goal. The user's
  goal (99 $) is about 70.191 JD at the 0.709 peg; he enters it himself.
- **Quick Log** (`ui/quicklog/QuickLogActivity.kt`): the add sheet (same `AddSheetContent`) over any app in its own
  translucent task, with the quick entries on top (one tap logs). Opened from the widgets' "+", the expense / income
  shortcuts and the AI Button. Saves with `created_by = QUICK` (or `WIDGET`), then a short toast and it closes. While
  the app lock is on it hides amounts and only adds; FLAG_SECURE follows the setting.
- **Widgets** (Glance 1.2.0, `ui/widgets/`): Balance (total, today's spending, "+"), Quick entries (2–3 per row, rows by
  height; one tap logs through `LogQuickAction` with a toast), My goal (pink, the featured goal). Amounts hidden when
  "Hide amounts" or the app lock is on. Redrawn on any wallet / settings / lock change (`AdamApp`, debounced 400 ms) and
  hourly. Cards follow the phone's dark mode; when the app's reading direction differs from the launcher's, rows and
  alignment are flipped by hand (`WidgetData.flip`).
- **App shortcuts** (`res/xml/shortcuts.xml`): New expense, New income (→ Quick Log), Count cash (→ the count screen),
  routed through the launcher router (`MainActivity` actions). Adaptive icons in the app's dark green.
- **AI Button double press**: Hyper Athlete's `LaunchRouter` ported (launch signature = referrer + icon bounds). Settings →
  Quick access → "Link the button", then double-press within two minutes; from then on that signature opens Quick Log,
  icon taps and notifications open the app. Stored on the phone only.
- Settings: one new group at the end, Quick access (AI Button card with steps and status, widgets and shortcuts help).
  Nothing else moved.
- Tests: 165 in all: 87 unit/Robolectric + 72 screenshots + 6 widget renders (Glance RemoteViews into PNGs). New: QuickAccessTest
  (router decisions, levels, featured pick rules, saved layout migration), LaunchRoutingTest (shortcuts, learning the
  button, icon taps), WidgetRenderTest.
- Unverified on device: the AI Button's double press on MagicOS (whether its launch is distinguishable), widgets on the
  Honor launcher (sizes, dark mode, one-tap logging), shortcut icons, Quick Log over other apps.
